## Read the evidence and terms

Read [credit profiles](/docs/concepts/credit-profiles): `onchainProfile` gives booked history and recorded
bond outcomes; `attestedProfile` gives a separately trusted assessment. Check `complete`, `bondsCreated`,
signer and expiry. Attributed revenue excludes detected related payers/unclassified transfers according
to indexer policy. A proven payer with no detected funding link is not necessarily an independent customer.

Read `BondrailBond.terms()`, `phase()`, `releasedPrincipal()`, `undrawnPrincipal()`, `pool()` and
`effectiveRevenueCap()`. First release is 25% or 50%, but more can leave after the milestone. Revenue
share can step up through `checkTrigger`; `revenueBps()` is the current share. A missed cap at maturity
is a loss even without a covenant default. SDK `impliedYield` projects trailing revenue; it is not a promise.

## Direct bond investment

Approve the bond for its asset, then call `deposit(amount)`. It requires Funding, a future funding
deadline, remaining raise capacity and `factory.depositAllowed(bond)`. Claims mint 1:1 and cannot
transfer during Funding. Before activation and before the deadline, `withdraw(amount, to)` burns
claims for escrowed assets 1:1, reopening the raise gap. It does not require a healthy source.

After the deadline, `account.cancelFunding(bond)` can cancel a still-Funding bond, then holders use
`bond.refund(amount, to)`. After activation, `redeem(tokens, to)` burns claims for their share of the
current pool; this gives up all future revenue and recoveries on those claims. Active or Closing redemption
cannot burn the last outstanding claim. `redeem` does not finalize settlement: call
`finalizeSettlement()` after `settleAfter` to make a Watched bond Closed. A nonzero pool payout
that rounds to zero reverts; when the pool itself is zero, redeeming can burn claims for no assets. Listed claims can trade on Kuru if a market and liquidity exist.

## Vault deposits and withdrawals

The [credit vault](/docs/concepts/credit-vault) pools the asset across curator-selected Funding bonds.
The `/vault` page displays prices and holdings; it is **read-only**. Use the contract or your integration
for the following ERC-4626 operations; no vault transaction UI is implied.

| Intent | Quote | Transaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deposit a chosen asset amount | `previewDeposit(assets)` | `deposit(assets, receiver)` after asset approval |
| Mint a chosen share amount | `previewMint(shares)` | `mint(shares, receiver)` after asset approval |
| Receive a chosen asset amount | `previewWithdraw(assets)` and `maxWithdraw(owner)` | `withdraw(assets, receiver, owner)` |
| Redeem a chosen share amount | `previewRedeem(shares)` and `maxRedeem(owner)` | `redeem(shares, receiver, owner)` |

Entries use `entryAssets()`; exits and `convertToAssets` / `convertToShares` use `totalAssets()` (exit NAV).
Use the entry previews for deposits/mints, especially during impairments. Withdrawals by a different
caller need a share allowance. A preview is not a liquidity reservation or slippage limit; simulate
before submission and protect price bounds in your integration where required.

Deposits remain open during Active/Closing bonds. Both prices exclude unvested profit, recognized over
seven days after observation; new profit can extend the remainder's vesting. Exit values apply trigger
and default/maturity write-downs. Final settlement losses affect both prices.

`maxWithdraw` / `maxRedeem` cap exits at idle cash. The vault does not sell or redeem active bonds to
fund your exit and has no withdrawal queue. Early exits can consume all cash, including the allocation
buffer. The curator only `collect`s Cancelled/Closed positions back into the vault; its ability to do
so and the issuers' performance affect later liquidity. Review the vault's full risk and power limits.
