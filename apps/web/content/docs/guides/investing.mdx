## Read the profile
- **Attributed vs raw revenue:** attributed revenue is payments with a proven payer (a Native pull from the
  payer's own balance, or an x402 payment the payer signed) and no detected link to the issuer: it excludes the
  issuer's own wallets, payers its treasury funded, and unclassified transfers. It does not prove that a payer is
  an independent customer or that a service was delivered; wallet diversity is not customer diversity, and the
  funding check sees one hop.
- **Payers and concentration:** how many distinct payers, and how much the largest one accounts for.
- **Age and consistency:** time since the first payment; steady daily revenue vs a spike before listing.
- **Track record:** earlier bonds on the same account and how they ended.

## Read the terms
- **Multiple:** `repayCap ÷ raise`. **Coverage:** projected repayment by maturity ÷ cap.
- **At risk now:** only the first release (25% or 50%) has left the bond; the rest returns to you if the
  milestone is missed.
- **Tier:** a Native bond's fees can't be diverted on the reviewed contract (the business can still stop or move
  users elsewhere); Watched bonds are monitored and backed by a stake. Check the stake against the investor share
  of a day's revenue: that is roughly what a diversion can cost before it defaults.
- **Asset:** the bond repays in its account's token.

## Fund, redeem, trade
Approve the bond for its asset and `deposit` while it is funding. After activation, `redeem` at any time for
your share of what has been collected (the last claim waits for close), or trade the claim on Kuru.
