## 1. Create the account

Create it on day one, in the token you charge fees in. History starts at the first payment.

```ts
import { sliceClient } from '@bondrail/sdk/client';
const slice = sliceClient({ publicClient, walletClient, factory });
const account = await slice.createAccount(USDC);
```

## 2. Inherit SliceEarner

`SliceEarner` fixes the account at deploy and gives you two calls, both in the account's asset:

<Source file="contracts/src/SliceEarner.sol" from="function _collect(" />
<Source file="contracts/src/SliceEarner.sol" from="function _settleOut(" />

Use `_collect` when the fee comes off what the payer sends (a buy), and `_settleOut` when it comes off what
the payer receives (a sell): the payout goes through the account, so the seller approves nothing. This is the
demo launchpad, PumpLab:

<Source file="demo/contracts/PumpLab.sol" from="function sell(" title="demo/contracts/PumpLab.sol (sell)" />

<Callout title="Rules the reviewer checks">
Every fee goes through `_collect` or `_settleOut`, in the account's asset (convert other tokens in your own
code first). No other path moves fees out. The contract is not upgradeable. Only the protocol's own cut is
revenue; LP fees and liquidity deposits do not go through the account.
</Callout>

## 3. Bind and get reviewed

```ts
await slice.bindProject(account, project); // issuer only, once; the project must point at the account
```

The disclosed reviewer then calls `factory.setNativeApproval(project, true)`. History accrues before review;
review is required to raise.

## 4. Payers approve the account, not your contract

For buys, payers approve (or permit) the **account** for the payment token. With EIP-2612 tokens, one
signature and no standing approval:

## 5. Protect investors with a fee floor

When you raise, set `minFeeBps` to your fee rate. While the bond is active, any payment below the floor
reverts, so investors know your fee cannot be cut after they fund you.
