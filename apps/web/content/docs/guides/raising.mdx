## Before raising

`BondrailFactory.createBond` requires the caller to own the account, no attached bond, nonzero policy
hash, valid terms and `minHistory()` since `firstRevenueAt()`. Read the factory value; the current manifest
configures 600 seconds for demonstrations. Booked history includes self-payments.

Native projects must be bound and approved, individually via `setNativeApproval` or through a still
approved template deployed with `deployNative`. Custom contracts need individual review; reviewed
templates need none. Watched bonds require nonzero stake and zero `minFeeBps`, with no Native source.
Approve the stake asset to the factory before creation. See [Native](/docs/guides/native) and [x402](/docs/guides/x402).

The app's signed profile and manifest establish listing metadata; they are separate from contract
issuance checks. Sign the profile in the [console](/console) to participate in its default market view.

## Choose terms and performance triggers

`terms()` fixes raise, repay cap, base share, stake, release schedule, milestone and optional fee floor.
`repayCap` must be at least the raise; the base investor share is positive and at most 50%. First release
is 25% or 50%. Effective revenue cap scales with principal actually released. Coverage and SDK `creditLine`
estimates use revenue assumptions; they are not verified future income or financing guarantees.

To disable the performance trigger, set `triggerRevenue`, `triggerWindowDays` and `stepUpBps` all to zero.
Otherwise choose a positive asset-unit revenue floor, a 1–30 day window, and a step-up share greater than
`revenueBps` and at most 5000 bps. `@bondrail/sdk/trigger.parseTriggerTerms` validates UI input.

Anyone can call `bond.checkTrigger()` once per UTC day while Active and not defaulted. It sums complete
UTC buckets in `account.revenueByDay`, excluding the activation day and today. The first eligible day is
`floor(activationTime / 86400) + 1 + triggerWindowDays`. Below the floor uses `stepUpBps`; equality or above
restores the base share. Payments use the last checked result until another check; they do not evaluate
it automatically. The cap does not change. Self-payments count; this is not wash-adjusted revenue.

## Fund, withdraw during the raise, then activate

```ts
import { bondrailClient, Tier } from '@bondrail/sdk/client';
const bondrail = bondrailClient({ publicClient, walletClient, factory });
const bond = await bondrail.createBond({ account, terms, tier: Tier.Native, source: project, policyHash, name, symbol });
// After investors fully fund the raise and the source passes the factory's health check:
await bondrail.activate(bond);
// Later, inside the release window and after the Onchain milestone:
await bondrail.releaseSecond(bond);
```

Investors call `bond.deposit(amount)` during Funding before the deadline. They may call
`bond.withdraw(amount, to)` before that deadline while still Funding, burning claims and taking their
escrow back 1:1. This reopens a funding gap, even if the raise was full; activation needs full funding
at execution. Withdrawal does not require a healthy route. After the deadline, a still-Funding bond
can be cancelled with `account.cancelFunding(bond)` and refunded with `bond.refund(amount, to)`.

`factory.activate` books uncollected pre-activation inflows for the issuer before revenue sharing starts.
Watched activation and second release require a fresh Compliant observation; deposits only reject
`covenant.inViolation`, so deposit eligibility is not proof of fresh compliance.

## Release the rest and withdraw earned revenue

`releaseSecond` uses the on-chain counter since activation plus the minimum operating period.
`releaseSecondSigned` uses the disclosed milestone signer's EIP-712 attestation instead. Both need a
healthy source, no default and the agreed release window. If the deadline passes, anyone can call
`bond.expireUndrawn()` to move the remaining principal to investor recovery.

`account.withdraw(to, amount)` pays the issuer's already-booked `issuerCredit`; it is distinct from an
investor's Funding withdrawal. Revenue sharing ends when cap collection closes or
`account.closeBondAtMaturity(bond)` closes collection at maturity. See [milestones](/docs/concepts/milestones)
and [defaults](/docs/concepts/defaults).
