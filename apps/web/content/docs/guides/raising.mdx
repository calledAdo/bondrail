## Before you can raise
- **History:** at least `minHistory` since your first booked payment (10 minutes on testnet).
- **Native:** your project is reviewer-approved and bound. **Watched:** approve a stake to the factory.
- **Profile:** sign your profile (name, links, use of funds) and, for x402, serve your manifest. Listings
  without a valid profile are hidden from the default market view.

## Choose terms
Coverage is what investors look at first: verified daily revenue × `revenueBps` × days to maturity, divided by
`repayCap`. Below 1.0× investors are projected to lose money; most bonds should list above 1.2×.

## Create, fund, activate

```ts
const bond = await slice.createBond({ account, terms, tier: Tier.Native, source: project, policyHash, name, symbol });
// investors deposit until the raise is full
await slice.activate(bond); // first release to you; credit mode starts
await slice.releaseSecond(bond); // later, once the milestone is met (Onchain mode)
```

Withdraw your share of revenue at any time with `slice.withdraw(account, to, amount)`.
