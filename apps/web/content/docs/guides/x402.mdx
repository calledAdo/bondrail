[x402](https://x402.org) lets any client pay per request: the server answers `402 Payment Required` with an
offer, the client signs an EIP-3009 USDC authorization, and a facilitator settles it on-chain, straight to the
offer's `payTo`. Point `payTo` at your BondrailAccount and every paid call becomes booked revenue.

## 3. What the monitor checks

On every check (every 30 minutes on testnet; each report covers one 30-second epoch) the observer and
Chainlink CRE independently fetch the manifest and every endpoint:

- every payment option must pay your account, in the committed asset and network, at or above the price floor
  (raising prices is fine);
- the manifest must keep naming your account and listing every covered endpoint; endpoints you add later are
  checked too.

A violation is re-checked straight away in the next epoch, and two adjacent violations default your bond and
slash your stake. So a diversion is caught within one check interval plus about a minute. A day of Unknown
reports (your offer unreachable) while the bond is active also defaults it, and an hour pauses deposits. A
monitor outage (no reports at all) never counts against you.

## 4. Buyers can verify you

`bondrailGuard` wraps any x402 client's fetch and refuses to sign an offer whose `payTo` is not your published
account (optionally confirmed on-chain). The manifest is your own claim, so a buyer paying a business it has
financed also **pins** what it expects from the chain: `pinFromBond` reads the bond's account, asset and chain,
and any offer or manifest that names another account (even another real Bondrail account), another asset or
chain, or arrives through a redirect to another host, is refused:
