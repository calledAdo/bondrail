---
title: How it works
description: From the first payment to a repaid bond.
---

## 1. The account builds history

A business creates its SliceAccount in the token it charges fees in (USDC, WETH, any ERC-20). From then on
every payment is booked on-chain: `cumulativeRevenue`, `cumulativeVolume`, `paymentCount`, `uniquePayers`,
`firstRevenueAt`, `revenueByDay`. With no bond, 100% of revenue is credited to the business (**passthrough**).

## 2. The business raises

After `minHistory` seconds of history the issuer creates a bond: raise amount, repayment cap, revenue share,
schedule and milestone. Investors deposit and receive claims 1:1. Every deposit re-checks the source.

## 3. Capital is released in two stages

At activation 25% or 50% of the raise goes to the business. The rest stays in the bond until a milestone
proves revenue since activation. If the milestone deadline passes, it goes back to investors.

## 4. Every payment repays

While the bond is active, each payment's `revenueBps` share goes straight to the bond (**credit mode**), up to
the cap. A rounding carry means micro-payments never round investors to zero.

**Optional performance trigger — not yet deployed:** at issuance the business can agree to a larger
investor share if booked revenue falls below a floor over 1–30 complete UTC days. Anyone can check
once per UTC day after the full window falls on or after the activation day; today's partial bucket
is excluded. A later check at or above the floor restores the base share. Both tiers use the same
on-chain check, outside payments, and the cap stays unchanged. The last checked share persists until
someone checks again. Issuer self-payments count: padding costs real money through the investor share,
but can avoid the trigger. See [the bond terms](/docs/concepts/bond).

## 5. The bond closes

At the cap or at maturity the bond closes and the account returns to passthrough. Holders redeem their share
of the pool. The closed bond stays on the business's profile as track record.

```
Funding ──activate──► Active ──cap or maturity──► Closed           (Native)
   │                    │                 └────► Closing ─120 s─► Closed   (Watched: late evidence window)
   └─deadline, unfilled─► Cancelled (refunds)     └─ two violations or 24 h dark ─► default (stake to investors)
```
