---
title: What is Bondrail
description: Raise capital through revenue bonds, inspect credit evidence, and invest directly or through a vault.
---

<Callout type="warn">Monad testnet prototype; not audited. Demonstration activity uses controlled
wallets. These docs describe `next`; the Metropolis submission (judged) remains a separate historical
deployment. See [selected addresses](/docs/reference/deployments).</Callout>

Bondrail lets businesses create a revenue account with `BondrailFactory.createAccount` and issue a
bond with `createBond`. Investors deposit the account's asset and receive ERC-20 claims. After
activation, an agreed share of connected revenue goes to the bond up to its effective cap; capital is
released in stages. Repayment depends on revenue, not promised interest or guaranteed principal.

Native projects route fees through `BondrailAccount.collect` / `settleOut`. Paid APIs can use
`account.settle` to verify an EIP-3009 payer and book/split immediately. `sweep` books plain transfers.
Each route builds history before or during a bond.

- **Native templates:** `deployNative` deploys and binds approved template code without individual review.
  Custom contracts inheriting `BondrailEarner` need individual approval before raising.
- **Performance triggers:** `checkTrigger` can raise the investor share when booked revenue falls below
  the agreed floor; a later check can restore it. Payments use the last checked share.
- **Credit vault:** `BondrailVault` pools investments with carried-cost valuation, separate entry/exit
  prices, seven-day income vesting and exits limited to idle cash.
- **Credit profiles:** `onchainProfile` exposes state and coverage; `attestedProfile` adds a separately
  trusted assessment. SDK credit-line and yield estimates are advisory.

Claims can transfer after Funding. `BondrailBond.redeem` burns the position for current pool backing,
giving up future receipts; the last claim waits for Closed. Kuru exits depend on a listed market and liquidity.
