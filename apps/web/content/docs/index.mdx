Bondrail was previously called Slice. The sources on `next` use `Bondrail*` contracts and `@bondrail/*` packages; the judged live deployment retains its historical names and signed domains.

Bondrail lets onchain businesses **issue revenue bonds** to raise capital. Investors fund a bond and
receive claim tokens. A fixed share of business revenue is routed toward repayment, up to the bond's cap.

Each issuer builds a revenue record through a **BondrailAccount** before issuing a **BondrailBond**. Investors
can inspect that record, the terms, and the repayment balance. Native payments split revenue within the
transaction; paid API receipts require a separate collection transaction. Repayment depends on revenue,
not a promise of fixed interest.

- **Credit history from the first payment.** Revenue, payers, volume and fee rate are on-chain counters, not
  claims in a pitch deck.
- **Repayment without trust in the issuer.** The split happens inside each payment. The business cannot route
  around it (Native) or is punished for routing around it (Watched).
- **Claims you can exit.** Bond claims are ERC-20s, redeemable at any time for their share of what has been
  collected, and tradable on Kuru.

## Two ways to connect revenue

| Tier | For | Why fees can't be diverted |
|---|---|---|
| **Native** | Contracts built on Bondrail (inherit `BondrailEarner`) | Every fee moves through the account, which is fixed into the contract. A disclosed reviewer checks the code before it can raise. |
| **Watched** | Paid HTTP APIs (x402) | The business publishes its account in `/.well-known/bondrail.json`. Chainlink CRE checks every paid endpoint every 30 minutes (each report covers one 30-second epoch); a confirmed diversion defaults the bond and slashes the issuer's stake. |
