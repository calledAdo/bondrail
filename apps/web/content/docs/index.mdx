---
title: What is Slice
description: Revenue bonds for onchain businesses on Monad. Your revenue is your credit.
---

Onchain businesses earn fees every day but cannot borrow against them. Slice gives every business a
**SliceAccount** from day one. The account records each payment, with its payer, long before the business
asks for money. When it raises, investors buy a **SliceBond**, and the same account automatically pays them a
fixed slice of every payment until they are repaid.

- **Credit history from the first payment.** Revenue, payers, volume and fee rate are on-chain counters, not
  claims in a pitch deck.
- **Repayment without trust in the issuer.** The split happens inside each payment. The business cannot route
  around it (Native) or is punished for routing around it (Watched).
- **Claims you can exit.** Bond claims are ERC-20s, redeemable at any time for their share of what has been
  collected, and tradable on Kuru.

## Two ways to connect revenue

| Tier | For | Why fees can't be diverted |
|---|---|---|
| **Native** | Contracts built on Slice (inherit `SliceEarner`) | Every fee moves through the account, which is fixed into the contract. A disclosed reviewer checks the code before it can raise. |
| **Watched** | Paid HTTP APIs (x402) | The business publishes its account in `/.well-known/slice.json`. Chainlink CRE checks every paid endpoint every 30 minutes (each report covers one 30-second epoch); a confirmed diversion defaults the bond and slashes the issuer's stake. |
