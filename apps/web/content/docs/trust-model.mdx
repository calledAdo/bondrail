<Callout title="Testnet">Bondrail runs on Monad testnet. All demo activity comes from team-controlled wallets.</Callout>

## Enforced by contracts
- Only the bound project can move payments through a Native account, and the account is immutable in it.
- The split to investors happens inside every payment; the issuer's share is pull-based, so a reverting
  wallet cannot block payments.
- Bond accounting: assets always cover every liability (tested invariant).

## Disclosed parties

| Party | Can | Cannot |
|---|---|---|
| **Native reviewer** | Approve or revoke a Native project's code. Revoking blocks new deposits, activation and the second release. | Stop the split of an active bond, or move funds. |
| **Milestone signer** | Attest wash-adjusted revenue for bonds that chose the Signed milestone mode. | Anything for bonds in On-chain milestone mode. |
| **Observer** | Sign x402 observations (Compliant, Violation, Unknown) delivered through Chainlink CRE. | Deliver an observation without CRE, or reuse one. |

## Residual risks
- A business can stop operating or move users to a new contract. Coverage, the cap, the undrawn principal and
  the Kuru price are the protection.
- An x402 business can show unguarded buyers a different `payTo`. Guarded buyers refuse to pay, and the bond
  page alerts when attributed x402 revenue collapses while monitoring reports compliant.
- Accounts may hold any token. A token the issuer can mint makes its history meaningless; the market's
  default view promotes well-known assets only.
