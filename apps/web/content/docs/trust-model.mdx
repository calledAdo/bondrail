## Enforced by contracts

`BondrailAccount.collect` / `settleOut` restrict Native routing to the bound project; `settle` verifies
a token authorization and records its payer. `_book` splits Active revenue up to the cap, while the
issuer pulls its credited share with `withdraw`. `BondrailBond` separates escrow, releases, revenue,
recoveries and stake. Exact-transfer checks reject transfer-tax behavior on supported pull paths;
they do not guarantee a token's value or prevent freezes/rebases.

## Disclosed parties

| Party | Powers and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Native reviewer | `setNativeApproval` / `setNativeTemplate` approve or revoke code. Factory checks can block issuance, deposits and releases; revocation does not stop an Active payment split. |
| Milestone signer | Signs `releaseSecondSigned` evidence; Onchain milestones use the account counter instead. |
| Observer and CRE/forwarder | `onReport` requires the pinned forwarder plus observer signature. The current simulation forwarder is not independent proof of a decentralized oracle decision. |
| Facilitator | Relays `account.settle`, paying gas from its wallet. Cannot change a signed recipient/amount; availability and gas budget still affect service. |
| Vault curator | `allocate`, `withdrawFromRaise`, `collect` within fixed asset, phase and allocation limits; no arbitrary withdrawal to the curator. |
| Vault owner | `proposeCurator` / `acceptCurator` after two days; no fund-transfer or upgrade power. |
| Credit attester | Signs underwriting assertions for `attest`; cannot alter account counters or bond state. |
| Registry owner | `proposeAttester` / `acceptAttester` after two days; rotation changes which stored signatures are valid. |

## Residual risks

A business can stop or move customers elsewhere. Native review is not a formal proof; monitoring sees
observed declared offers and cannot guarantee what every customer sees. Missing reports can prevent
release without establishing default. Principal already released cannot be clawed back.

Booked revenue can be self-funded. Indexer attribution uses limited funding-link evidence; distinct
wallets need not be independent customers. `onchainProfile.complete` detects missing factory bonds
within its scope; `attestedProfile.valid` means current signer and unexpired, not true or complete evidence.

Vault carried value can exceed realized backing; exits depend on idle cash. Credit-line and yield
estimates depend on revenue assumptions. Claim markets need buyers and liquidity. See
[credit vault risks](/docs/concepts/credit-vault), [credit profiles](/docs/concepts/credit-profiles)
and [investing](/docs/guides/investing).
