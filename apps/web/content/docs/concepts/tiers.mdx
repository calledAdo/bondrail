title: Native and Watched
description: Reviewed contract routing and monitored HTTP payment offers have different trust boundaries.

Monad testnet; not audited. Both tiers use the same bond accounting and can opt into `checkTrigger`.

## Native

A project inherits `BondrailEarner`, whose account reference is immutable, and routes every fee through
`_collect` or `_settleOut`. Custom contracts need individual `setNativeApproval` before raising.
An approved creation-code template deployed through `factory.deployNative` needs no individual review.
`createBond`, `depositAllowed`, `activate` and the release checks verify approval and account binding.
See the [Native guide](/docs/guides/native).

Review is a trust assumption: inheriting the base does not itself rule out alternate fee paths or
upgrade powers. Routing is enforced for the reviewed path; the business can still stop operating or
move users elsewhere. `minFeeBps` constrains Native fees while Active, not future demand.

## Watched

A paid HTTP API publishes its account and endpoints in `/.well-known/bondrail.json`. An
account-mediated facilitator calls `account.settle` for immediate booking. Observer and CRE checks
compare observed offers with the policy committed by `policyHash`:

- **Violation:** a conflicting recipient/network/asset/scheme, a price below the floor, or a manifest
  that changes the account or drops covered endpoints.
- **Unknown:** the offer cannot be read or parsed.
- **Compliant:** observed offers satisfy the committed route.

`BondrailCovenant.onReport` checks the pinned forwarder and observer signature. Reports use 30-second
epochs; this is not a guarantee of a check every 30 seconds. Two qualifying adjacent violations can
record default; sustained Unknown reports have separate warning/default thresholds. See
[defaults and recovery](/docs/concepts/defaults). Monitoring cannot discover every undeclared endpoint
or prove that every customer sees the same offer.

Watched bonds require stake. `depositAllowed` rejects `inViolation`; activation and second release
require `isHealthy`, a fresh Compliant observation. Missing reports can block those releases without
establishing issuer default. [Guarded buyers](/docs/guides/x402) can independently pin a bond's route.
