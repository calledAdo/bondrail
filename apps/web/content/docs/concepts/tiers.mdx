---
title: Native and Watched
description: How each tier makes diversion impossible or expensive.
---

## Native: impossible by construction
The project inherits `SliceEarner`, which fixes its SliceAccount at deploy. Every fee moves through
`_collect` or `_settleOut`. The issuer binds the project to the account once, and a disclosed reviewer
approves the code before it can raise. Before every deposit, activation and second release the factory checks
that the project is still approved, bound and pointing at the account.

## Watched: caught and punished
For revenue paid over HTTP (x402). The business sets its `payTo` to the account and publishes
`/.well-known/slice.json`. Every 30 seconds the observer and Chainlink CRE read the manifest and every paid
endpoint independently:

- **Violation:** any payment option pays someone else, uses another network, asset or scheme, or is priced
  below the floor; or the manifest names another account or drops a covered endpoint.
- **Unknown:** nothing readable (outage or error).
- **Compliant:** everything pays the account.

Two adjacent violations default the bond. A day of continuous Unknowns while active also defaults it; an hour
pauses deposits. A Watched bond requires a stake, slashed to investors on default.
