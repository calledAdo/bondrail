---
title: BondrailAccount
description: The business's revenue address, payment entry point and booked credit history.
---

Monad testnet; not audited. `factory.createAccount(asset)` creates an account for one issuer and one
asset. An issuer may create multiple accounts; the protocol does not enforce one account per business.
Each account has at most one attached bond, and its bonds fund, repay and take stake in that asset.

## Payment routes

| Function | Caller | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `collect(payer, gross, fee)` | Bound Native project | Pulls gross from payer, books fee, sends net to project; payer approves account |
| `collectWithPermit(...)` | Bound Native project | Same pull with an EIP-2612 permit |
| `settleOut(payer, gross, fee)` | Bound Native project | Pulls gross from project, pays net to recipient and books fee |
| `settle(from, value, validAfter, validBefore, nonce, v, r, s)` | Any relayer | Verifies token EIP-3009 authorization through the account, emits `Settled`, books/splits the full payment |
| `sweep()` | Anyone | Books uncredited asset balance from plain transfers with no proven on-chain payer |

`BondrailEarner` fixes its account at deployment. `bindProject` checks that the project points back to
this account. Binding alone is not reviewer approval. Native routes authenticate callers and enforce
the Active bond's `minFeeBps`; `settle` treats the whole payment as revenue and has no Native fee floor.

## Modes and history

With no Active bond (including Funding), `_book` credits 100% of revenue to `issuerCredit`.
With an Active bond it splits `revenueBps()` to the bond, limited by `remainingRevenueCap()`, using a
rounding carry. The issuer calls `withdraw(to, amount)` for its available booked credit.

Getters include `cumulativeRevenue`, `cumulativeVolume`, `paymentCount`, `collectedCount`, `uniquePayers`,
`firstRevenueAt`, `lastRevenueAt` and `revenueByDay(day)`. Only nonzero booked revenue advances payment
history; zero-fee Native activity can increase volume without adding a revenue payment. Plain transfers
count on the day swept and do not increase proven-payer or volume counters.

These counters include self-payments. A pulled/authorized payer is not proof of an independent customer;
`settleOut` identifies the payment recipient. The [credit profile](/docs/concepts/credit-profiles) keeps
these facts distinct from indexer attribution and attested underwriting.
