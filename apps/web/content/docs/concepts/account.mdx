---
title: BondrailAccount
description: The business's revenue address, payment entrypoint and credit history.
---

One account per business, created by `factory.createAccount(asset)`. It holds one asset: the token the
business charges fees in. Its bonds raise, repay and take stake in the same token.

## Three ways money moves through it

| Path | Who calls | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `collect(payer, gross, fee)` | The bound Native project | Pulls `gross` from the payer, keeps `fee`, sends the rest to the project. The payer is proven by the pull. |
| `settleOut(payer, gross, fee)` | The bound Native project | Pulls `gross` from the project, pays `gross − fee` to the payer, keeps `fee`. For fees on what the payer receives. |
| `sweep()` | Anyone | Books any balance that arrived by plain transfer (x402 settlements, anything else). |

## Modes
- **Passthrough** (no bond, or the bond is still funding): 100% of revenue is credited to the issuer.
- **Credit** (bond active): `revenueBps` of each payment goes to the bond, capped at its remaining cap.

The issuer withdraws its share with `withdraw(to, amount)` at any time.

## History
`cumulativeRevenue`, `cumulativeVolume`, `paymentCount`, `collectedCount`, `uniquePayers`, `firstRevenueAt`,
`lastRevenueAt`, `revenueByDay(day)`. Fee rate = revenue ÷ volume for collected payments.

<Callout type="warn">The contract books every transfer it receives. The indexer separates proven payments
(Native pulls, x402 signatures) from unclassified inflows, which never count as verified revenue.</Callout>
