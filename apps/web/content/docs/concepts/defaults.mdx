---
title: Defaults and recovery
description: Contract-triggered recovery, evidence windows and the limits of investor protection.
---

Monad testnet; not audited. These outcomes are distinct:

| Event / function | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Missed milestone deadline: `bond.expireUndrawn()` | Moves undrawn principal to investor recovery; no clawback of released capital |
| Watched default: covenant calls `bond.markDefault(evidenceHash)` | Records default, recovers undrawn principal and slashes held stake into the pool |
| Still Funding after deadline: `account.cancelFunding(bond)` | Cancels the raise; holders call `bond.refund` 1:1; stake becomes issuer return credit |
| Maturity: `account.closeBondAtMaturity(bond)` | Ends collection, recovers undrawn principal and closes or starts the Watched evidence grace |
| End of grace: `bond.finalizeSettlement()` | Moves Closing to Closed and credits remaining stake for issuer return |

`BondrailCovenant.onReport` requires the pinned forwarder and observer signature. Two qualifying
Violation reports in adjacent 30-second epochs must refer to the active collection period. Watched
bonds remain in Closing for 120 seconds to accept eligible late evidence from before collection closed.
A run of Unknown reports with gaps no longer than one hour blocks deposits after one hour; after
24 hours during Active it can default. No reports alone do not establish a dark period or default.
The contract's evidence rules do not guarantee reporter availability or detection latency.

A performance trigger (`checkTrigger`) increases revenue share; it does not by itself set `defaultedAt`
or slash stake. A bond can close at maturity below its cap without a routing breach. Already released
capital cannot be recovered by these contracts. Remaining pool assets and stake may not cover losses.

`redeem` burns claims, including during Active/Closing, and gives up future recoveries on those claims.
The last outstanding claim waits for Closed. The vault has stricter behavior: `BondrailVault.collect`
only burns positions after Cancelled/Closed. ERC-8004 feedback is a separate explicit script action,
not an automatic effect of contract default.
