---
title: Defaults and recovery
description: What investors get back when things go wrong.
---

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Milestone missed | Undrawn capital becomes investor recovery |
| Watched default (two adjacent violations, or 24 h dark while active) | Stake slashed into the pool; undrawn capital to recovery; ERC-8004 feedback for agents |
| Funding deadline unfilled | Bond cancelled; investors refund 1:1; stake returned |
| Maturity | Bond closes; holders redeem the pool |

Recovery never claws back capital already released to the business. Investors are protected by what is still
in the bond and by the stake.
