## Two separate trust layers

`onchainProfile(account)` reads the immutable factory's account and recorded bonds directly. No credit
attester is needed to read those counters. It returns asset, issuer, bound project, history age,
`cumulativeRevenue`, `cumulativeVolume`, `paymentCount`, `collectedCount`, `uniquePayers`, bond outcomes
and repayment totals. `tier` is Native if a project is bound, otherwise Watched if bonds are recorded,
otherwise Unknown. It does not assert current reviewer approval or every historical bond's adapter.

These are trustless reads of recorded state, not proof of economic quality. Booked revenue includes
self-payments and swept transfers. Unique payer addresses are not independent customers. The default
flag records the covenant's decision and inherits its observer/CRE trust.

`attestedProfile(account)` separately returns a `CreditAttestation`, its original signer and `valid`:

| Attested field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `attributedRevenue`, `excludedRevenue` | The signer's revenue attribution and exclusion analysis |
| `distinctPayers`, `topPayerShareBps` | The signer's eligible payer count and concentration |
| `creditLine` | Advisory amount in asset base units under the signer's credit policy |
| `periodStart`, `periodEnd`, `issuedAt`, `expiresAt` | Assessment period and signature validity window |
| `evidenceHash`, `chainId`, `nonce` | Commitment to evidence, chain binding and replay protection |

The signer is trusted for indexer completeness, issuer-funding links and exclusion policy. The hash
commits to evidence; it does not prove truth or availability. `attest` checks the EIP-712 domain
(`BondrailCreditRegistry`, version `1`, chain and registry), current signer, time bounds, nonzero evidence,
concentration bounds and nonce. A newer period wins; equal-period corrections need a later `issuedAt`.

## Check completeness every time

Anyone can `recordBond(bond)` for a factory bond. Membership is unique, append-only and capped at **64
bonds per account**. `recordedBonds(account)` lists that membership. The repayment summary covers only
those bonds; `factory.bondsCreated(account)` counts all successful issuance, including cancelled and
defaulted bonds. `onchainProfile.complete` compares the recorded count with that lifetime count.

Display **“Repayment record covers N of M bonds”**, using `repayment.bondsIssued` and `bondsCreated`.
When `complete` is false, omitted bonds may include defaults. Zero of zero is complete; new issuance
makes coverage incomplete until recorded. At 64 of 65, the registry cannot become complete because
of its bound. Coverage is specific to this account, factory and chain.

`totalRaised` sums original raises only after activation. `totalCollected` is revenue received;
`totalRepaidToInvestors` sums actual `totalRedeemed`, including principal/stake recoveries. Unclaimed
pool backing is not yet paid out. A RevenueCap close is counted as fully repaid even during Closing;
the default flag can overlap it. `repaidRatioBps` compares collected revenue with the effective revenue
cap, excluding principal/stake recovery. Inspect per-bond phase, close reason and default together.

## Rotation and expiry

The immutable owner calls `proposeAttester(next)` then `acceptAttester()` after **two days**; replacing
a proposal restarts the delay. A proposal alone does not change validity. After accepting a different
signer, old stored assessments remain readable but `valid` is false. Expiry also invalidates them.
Consumed nonces remain used across rotation. Valid means signed by the **current** attester and unexpired,
not independently verified. Show the original signer and expiry even when invalid.

## How another lender reads it

```ts
import { getCreditProfile, creditAttestationLabel } from '@bondrail/sdk/credit';

const profile = await getCreditProfile(publicClient, registry, account);
console.log(profile.onchain.repayment.bondsIssued, profile.onchain.bondsCreated);
if (!profile.onchain.complete) throw new Error('Incomplete repayment history');
console.log(creditAttestationLabel(profile, BigInt(Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000))));
```

The helper reads both layers and the current attester at one block. Solidity lenders can use
`IBondrailCreditRegistry` from `@bondrail/contracts` and call `onchainProfile` / `attestedProfile`.
Pin the registry and check `factory()`, asset, coverage, freshness and signer against your own policy.
Neither `creditLine` nor this example enforces a safe lending limit.

The [credit design](https://github.com/calledAdo/bondrail/blob/main/docs/credit/DESIGN.md) documents the
attester's canonical evidence and backfill procedure. Its original pre-deployment status is historical;
current behavior is in [`BondrailCreditRegistry.sol`](https://github.com/calledAdo/bondrail/blob/main/contracts/src/BondrailCreditRegistry.sol).
