<Callout type="warn">Monad testnet; not audited. The current manifest includes a `BondrailVault`.

deposits and withdrawals are contract calls.</Callout>

`BondrailVault` accepts one exact-transfer, non-rebasing asset and issues transferable ERC-4626 shares.
The curator uses `allocate(bond, amount)` to fund eligible bonds from its immutable factory. Share value
includes cash and carried bond positions; it is not a guaranteed redemption balance.

## Carried cost and losses

`costBasis(bond)` records allocation cost. `holdingRealizedValue(bond)` is the vault's claims'
pro-rata share of the current bond `pool()` (1:1 claims during Funding/Cancelled).
`holdingRecovered(bond)` uses cumulative revenue, principal recovery and stake recovery for the current
claims and supply. Unrecovered cost is cost basis minus recovered value, floored at zero.

`holdingEntryValue` and `holdingValue` apply these rules before the vault-wide income deduction:

| Bond condition | Entry value | Exit value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Funding / Cancelled | Realized backing | Realized backing |
| Active, healthy, before maturity | Realized + unrecovered cost | Same |
| Active, `triggered()` | Realized + unrecovered cost | Realized + 50% of unrecovered cost |
| Active, defaulted or past maturity | Realized + unrecovered cost | Realized + remaining cost written down linearly over 30 days |
| Closing / Closed | Realized backing only | Realized backing only |

Active values are capped at the position's share of `effectiveRevenueCap + undrawnPrincipal +
principalRecovery + stakeRecovery - totalRedeemed`. This includes refundable principal separately
from the revenue cap, which scales with released capital. Unslashed issuer stake is excluded.

The write-down starts at `defaultedAt()` or maturity, whichever applies first; a late default cannot
restart it. The default/maturity rule takes precedence over the trigger haircut and starts with zero
time-based haircut. A fresh default can therefore initially increase a previously triggered position's
carrying value; this is not a monotonic risk score. Repayment shrinks unrecovered cost and increases
backing. A later `checkTrigger()` can clear the trigger haircut, but default flags do not clear.

## Entry price differs from exit price

`entryAssets()` ignores unrealized trigger/default impairments; `totalAssets()` is **exit NAV**, including
those impairments. Both subtract `unvestedIncome()`. Final Closing/Closed losses affect both.

- Quote `deposit(assets, receiver)` with `previewDeposit` (shares round down).
- Quote `mint(shares, receiver)` with `previewMint` (assets round up).
- Quote `withdraw` / `redeem` with `previewWithdraw` / `previewRedeem` at exit NAV.
- `convertToAssets` and `convertToShares` use exit NAV; they are not entry quotes when impaired.

Entries stay open throughout bond lifecycles. Entering at the higher price prevents new deposits buying
unrealized impairments at the exit discount; it does not protect entrants from final losses. OpenZeppelin
virtual assets/shares and a six-decimal offset affect rounding (a six-decimal asset has 12-decimal shares).
Zero-asset or zero-share movements revert. Transactions have no built-in user slippage argument.

## Seven-day income vesting

Profit is realized backing above cost, not projected revenue. `unvestedIncome()` excludes all newly
observed profit from both prices, including profit not yet checkpointed. Anyone can call
`checkpointIncome()`; successful vault money movements also checkpoint. New profit merges with the
still-unvested remainder and starts a fresh **seven-day** linear schedule. With no new profit, a
checkpoint does not restart time. Reading a price alone does not start vesting.

Vesting starts when a write observes profit, not at its original payment block. Later inflows can
extend the old remainder's tail; fully vested income is not relocked. `collect()` moves Closed backing
to idle cash without unlocking profit early. This is not an entitlement snapshot: new holders who
stay invested participate as income vests. Direct asset donations affect price immediately.

## Curator powers and hard limits

`allocate` only accepts same-asset, factory-registered Funding bonds with `depositAllowed(bond) == true`.
At each allocation, fixed limits enforce **25% of pre-allocation exit NAV per bond**, **16 holdings**,
and **20% of exit NAV remaining idle**. These are allocation checks; withdrawals, losses and recoveries
can change concentrations and consume the buffer. There is no issuer-diversification guarantee.

The curator can `withdrawFromRaise` while bond withdrawal is allowed, or `collect` entire positions
after Cancelled/Closed. It cannot redeem Active/Closing claims, sell claims, call arbitrary contracts,
rescue assets or charge fees. The immutable owner's only administrative power is `proposeCurator` and
`acceptCurator` after two days; replacing a proposal restarts that delay. There is no upgrade or pause.

## Liquidity and risks

`maxWithdraw(holder)` and `maxRedeem(holder)` limit exits to idle cash. A preview gives a price, not an
availability promise. There is no queue or automatic bond liquidation; first exits can exhaust cash.
Even realized bond backing can remain unavailable until cancellation or final settlement.

Carrying at cost assumes healthy bonds keep paying. Exit NAV can exceed pool backing and is neither a
market quote nor a liquidation floor. Consider issuer losses/correlation, curator availability,
reviewer/observer trust, delayed trigger checks/default reports, settlement pricing, vesting timing,
token freezes, donations, rounding and dust. No principal or yield is guaranteed.
