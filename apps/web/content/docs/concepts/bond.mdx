## Terms (fixed at creation)

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `raiseAmount` | Capital raised; claims mint 1:1 |
| `repayCap` | Most the bond can collect (≥ raise). Scales with released capital: `repayCap × released ÷ raise`. |
| `revenueBps` | Investors' share of each payment while active (≤ 50%) |
| `firstReleaseBps` | 25% or 50% released at activation |
| `minRevenue`, `minOperatingPeriod` | The milestone for the second release |
| `fundingDeadline`, `secondReleaseNotBefore`, `secondReleaseDeadline`, `maturity` | The schedule |
| `stake` | Issuer stake, slashed to investors on default (required for Watched) |
| `milestoneMode` | `Onchain` or `Signed` |
| `minFeeBps` | Native fee floor while active (0 = none) |

## Lifecycle
- **Funding:** deposits until the raise is full; claims cannot be transferred; refunds if the deadline passes unfilled.
- **Active:** revenue repays; claims trade; `redeem(tokens, to)` pays `tokens × pool ÷ supply` at any time.
  The last claim waits for Closed.
- **Closing → Closed:** at the cap or maturity. Watched bonds keep a 120 s window for late evidence.
- Undrawn principal becomes investor recovery if the milestone deadline passes or the bond defaults.
