## Terms (fixed at creation)

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `raiseAmount` | Capital raised; claims mint 1:1 |
| `repayCap` | Most the bond can collect (≥ raise). Scales with released capital: `repayCap × released ÷ raise`. |
| `revenueBps` | Base investor share of each payment while active (≤ 50%) |
| `firstReleaseBps` | 25% or 50% released at activation |
| `minRevenue`, `minOperatingPeriod` | The milestone for the second release |
| `fundingDeadline`, `secondReleaseNotBefore`, `secondReleaseDeadline`, `maturity` | The schedule |
| `stake` | Issuer stake, slashed to investors on default (required for Watched) |
| `milestoneMode` | `Onchain` or `Signed` |
| `minFeeBps` | Native fee floor while active (0 = none) |
| `triggerRevenue`, `triggerWindowDays`, `stepUpBps` | Optional performance trigger: all zero disables, otherwise positive revenue floor over 1–30 days and a share above the base, at most 50% |

## Performance trigger

Anyone can call `checkTrigger()` once per UTC day while Active and not defaulted. It sums the last
`triggerWindowDays` complete UTC buckets in the account's `revenueByDay` mapping, excluding today.
Every bucket must fall strictly after the activation UTC day; receipts before and after activation
on that day are excluded. The first eligible UTC day is `activationDay + 1 + triggerWindowDays`,
where `activationDay = floor(activationTime / 86400)`. Below `triggerRevenue` selects `stepUpBps`;
equality or above restores the base share.
Payments use the last checked share until another check. Rounding carry and the cap are unchanged.
`revenueBps()` is the effective share; `baseRevenueBps()` and `terms().revenueBps` retain the base.

Issuer self-payments count. Padding revenue to avoid a trigger costs real money because investors
receive their share, but is possible. This counter is not wash-adjusted; plain transfers count when
swept. The next deployment includes this implementation. Historical immutable bonds do not gain the feature.

## Lifecycle
- **Funding:** `deposit` until full before the deadline; `withdraw` takes escrow back before that deadline; claims cannot transfer. A still-Funding bond can be cancelled after the deadline and holders call `refund`.
- **Active:** revenue repays; claims trade; `redeem(tokens, to)` burns claims for `tokens × pool ÷ supply`, giving up future receipts.
  The last claim waits for Closed.
- **Closing → Closed:** at the cap or maturity. Watched bonds keep a 120 s window for late evidence, then need `finalizeSettlement()`. Partial redemption also works during Closing; the last claim waits for Closed.
- Undrawn principal becomes investor recovery if the milestone deadline passes or the bond defaults.
