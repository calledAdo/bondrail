## Bondrail's treatment

Open on the actual landing page. Establish the tension between revenue arriving today and capital needed for growth. Follow PumpLab from its inspected history to staged funding, then the single real transaction that repays the bond. Hand the story to the investor: trade a claim or join an open raise. Show how the second tier extends to paid APIs. Return to the landing promise.

Visual tokens: ink `#202A27`, paper `#F6F5EF`, mint `#9FE8CA`, muted ink `#66746e`, white `#ffffff`. These derive from Bondrail's existing black/white/emerald identity. Geist carries headlines, UI and supporting prose; Geist Mono is reserved for transaction identifiers and evidence numbers. Large sentence-case headlines; one main claim per shot. No recurring oversized chapter banner.

Composition alternates actual full-screen browser hero, app-plus-explanation frames, a single brief conceptual contrast, and a large real receipt. A recurring quarter-circle slice connects the logo to revenue splitting. Smooth camera pushes and pans follow the source data. Light frames make dark app evidence readable; mint marks the funding and repayment payoffs. Evidence numbers appear only after the supporting action/state; decorative counters never replace app values.

The early concept explanation is clearly separate from the actual app. The majority of the running time shows genuine product and chain evidence. All transaction footage comes from the original successful takes. Any shortened wait/held final frame is disclosed; no repeated trades, fabricated account states or live Oracle default.
