# Bondrail introduction — working script

Target:105–115seconds with male synthetic narration. Final team and personal-motivation lines depend on the user's reply. Bracketed text is a writing placeholder, never final narration.

**Opening / problem**

Your business earns onchain. But its next move needs capital today.

An audit. More capacity. A new launch.

Revenue arrives over time. The next bill is due now.

That's why we're building Bondrail. Revenue bonds for onchain businesses. Raise capital from investors, and repay from a share of revenue.

**Team / personal motivation — awaiting facts**

[Introduce each public name and role, or the solo founder. One concise sentence.]

[One concrete, true sentence about the founder's motivation or the problem they encountered. Do not invent professional experience, customer interviews, financing difficulties or traction.]

**Product belief / solution**

Our starting point is simple: a business's onchain revenue should help investors understand what they're funding. And repayment should follow rules they can inspect.

With Bondrail, the business sets its raise, revenue share and repayment cap. Investors fund the bond and receive claims. Capital can release in stages, with the rest held behind a milestone.

**Evidence / why Monad**

We've built a working prototype on Monad testnet. Here, a bond is issued, funded and activated through the app.

For a Native integration, a business payment routes a share of its fee to the bond in the same transaction. The repayment is visible onchain.

These are controlled testnet demonstrations. The next step is putting the product in the hands of real businesses.

**First users / close**

We'll start with revenue-generating apps on Monad, help our first businesses issue a bond, and reach investors through their communities.

We're building toward a simple outcome: businesses can fund their next move, with revenue at the centre of the relationship.

Bondrail. Issue bonds. Raise capital. Repay investors from your revenue.
