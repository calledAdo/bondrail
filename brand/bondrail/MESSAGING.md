# Bondrail messaging

## Core positioning

**Revenue bonds for onchain businesses.**

Bondrail lets onchain businesses issue revenue bonds to raise capital. Investors fund the bonds and receive claim tokens; a share of business revenue is routed toward repayment, up to the bond's cap.

## Message hierarchy

- Category: **Revenue bonds for onchain businesses.**
- Headline: **Issue bonds. Raise capital.**
- Supporting line: **Repay investors from your revenue.**
- Issuer action: **Issue a bond**.
- Investor action: **Explore bonds**.
- Developer action: **Integrate Bondrail**.

Lead with the instrument, then the benefit and repayment mechanism. Revenue history supports underwriting; it is not the headline product. Credit scoring, staged capital releases, trading, and monitoring explain how the bonds work.

## By audience

**Businesses:** Issue a revenue bond, set the terms, and raise capital from investors. Route a share of revenue toward repayment.

**Investors:** Assess business revenue and bond terms, fund a bond, and receive claims on collected repayments. Eligible bond claims can trade on Kuru, subject to liquidity.

**Developers:** Integrate revenue collection, bond issuance, and repayment into an onchain business or paid API.

## Product precision

- Always qualify the instrument as a **revenue bond** in introductions. These are capped revenue claims, not a promise of fixed coupons or guaranteed returns.
- Native integrations split fees in the same transaction. Paid API receipts need a separate collection transaction; monitoring checks payment destinations.
- A revenue record helps investors assess a business and informs suggested terms. It does not guarantee funding, repayment, or a credit rating.
- Claims can trade on supported markets; do not imply guaranteed liquidity or an immediate exit.
- Identify current demos as Monad testnet activity from controlled wallets.
- Keep protocol identifiers and technical credit calculations accurate. This is a positioning change, not new contract functionality.
